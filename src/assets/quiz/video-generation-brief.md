# Puppy family video

Reference image: `puppy-family.png` in this directory.

Recreate this photorealistic outdoor puppy nursery as a short, smoothly looping
video. Preserve the white whelping pen, grass, warm natural light, two adult
golden dogs behind the pen, and seven puppies inside it.

Both adult dogs must move naturally: gentle breathing, blinking, small head
turns toward their puppies, and relaxed tail wagging. Keep their paws planted
and their anatomy consistent. Puppies gently play, look around, and shift their
weight; the sleeping puppy breathes softly. Keep motion calm and believable.

Use a locked, wide camera with no zoom, pan, or tilt. Both adult dogs must stay
fully visible from ears to paws, including their tails, for the entire clip.
Leave generous space above their heads and around the whole pen. Extend the
grass at the sides if a wider format is required; do not crop the reference.
Preserve the pen geometry, accessories, and existing branding without changing
or adding text. No extra animals, merged bodies, warped limbs, flicker, or cuts.

Target: 8–10 seconds, landscape, at least 1080p if available, silent playback.
Prefer the original image aspect ratio if supported. Otherwise use 16:9 with
expanded scenery, preserving the entire family and pen. Match the first and
last pose as closely as possible for looping.

Before integration, inspect the beginning, middle, and end for full parent-dog
visibility, natural motion, and stable geometry. Replace the composite scene
with the new single video only after these checks pass. Retain a still poster,
pause/resume controls, reduced-motion behavior, and responsive containment.
